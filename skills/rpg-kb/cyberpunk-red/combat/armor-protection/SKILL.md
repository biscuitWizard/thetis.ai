# Cyberpunk RED armor protection

Protection is tracked for the area a hit reaches. It can stop an attack that does not overcome its rating; stronger damage passes through to the wearer after protection is accounted for. A penetrating hit can also reduce future protection. Separate coverage and special armor interactions require the licensed rules, and exact ratings belong in reviewed lookup tables rather than this summary.
