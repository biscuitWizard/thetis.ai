# Human Shields are a unique form of cover in combat, derived from

Human Shields are a unique form of cover in combat, derived from grappling an opponent. They offer cover but can only block certain ranged attacks and cannot deflect melee attacks. Using a human as a shield against ranged attacks not aimed at the head negates their ability to dodge and makes them vulnerable to damage. When they die, they continue to serve as a shield with HP equal to their BODY stat until dropped, which requires an Action.
