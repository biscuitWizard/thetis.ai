# Armor in Night City is categorized by its ability to stop damage,

Armor in Night City is categorized by its ability to stop damage, known as Stopping Power (SP). The highest SP value in a location is the only one that counts, even if multiple pieces of armor are worn there. When damaged, all armor in a location is ablated, reducing its SP.

Source path for review: Cyberpunk RED Core Book > Combat > Basic Driving [summary; loc 435854].
