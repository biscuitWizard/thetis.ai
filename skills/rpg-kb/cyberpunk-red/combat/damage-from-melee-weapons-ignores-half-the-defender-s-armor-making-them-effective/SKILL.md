# Damage from melee weapons ignores half the defender's armor, making them effective

Damage from melee weapons ignores half the defender's armor, making them effective in close combat situations despite the defender's armor rating. The number of attack strikes varies based on the weapon type.

Source path for review: Cyberpunk RED Core Book > Combat > Armor Breaking Combination [summary; loc 400117].
