# Falling from a height results in 2d6 damage per 10 meters fallen

Falling from a height results in 2d6 damage per 10 meters fallen if a character fails an Athletics check. Falls can also lead to a Broken Leg Critical Injury unless the character has Cyberlegs.

Source path for review: Cyberpunk RED Core Book > Combat > Critical Injuries to the Body [summary; loc 421515].
