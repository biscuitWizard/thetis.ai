# Martial Arts in "Friday Night Firefight" employ the Martial Arts Skill for

Martial Arts in "Friday Night Firefight" employ the Martial Arts Skill for specialized attacks, with an attack rate of 2 ROF, and require at least one point in the skill. The damage from Martial Arts Attacks is determined by the character's BODY stat, ignoring half of the defender's armor.
