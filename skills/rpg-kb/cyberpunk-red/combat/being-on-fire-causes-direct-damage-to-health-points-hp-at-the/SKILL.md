# Being on fire causes direct damage to health points (HP) at the

Being on fire causes direct damage to health points (HP) at the end of a turn, depending on the intensity of the fire: mild causes 2 damage, strong results in 4, and deadly inflicts 6. Armor does not protect against this damage.

Source path for review: Cyberpunk RED Core Book > Combat > Critical Injuries to the Body [summary; loc 421515].
