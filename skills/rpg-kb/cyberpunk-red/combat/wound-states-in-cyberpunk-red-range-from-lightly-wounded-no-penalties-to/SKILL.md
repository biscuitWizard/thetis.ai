# Wound States in Cyberpunk Red range from Lightly Wounded (no penalties) to

Wound States in Cyberpunk Red range from Lightly Wounded (no penalties) to Dead. As health drops, penalties to actions and increased risks of death save rolls intensify; Mortally Wounded characters must roll death saves and suffer critical injuries more frequently.
