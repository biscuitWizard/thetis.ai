# Shields serve as portable cover in combat

Shields serve as portable cover in combat. Equipping or dropping a shield requires an Action, and while using a shield, it cannot be used for anything else. Shields absorb the entire attack until their HP depletes to 0, upon which they are rendered unusable for cover until repaired. Using a shield negates the ability to dodge, although ranged attacks can still miss due to poor aim.
