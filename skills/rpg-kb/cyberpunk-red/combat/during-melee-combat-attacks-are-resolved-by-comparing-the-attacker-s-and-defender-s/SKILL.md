# During melee combat, attacks are resolved by comparing the attacker's and defender's

During melee combat, attacks are resolved by comparing the attacker's and defender's rolls using Dexterity and relevant skills along with a d10 roll. Heavy melee weapons deal substantial damage but cannot be used for multiple attacks in a single action, while lighter weapons may be used for multiple strikes.
