# Resolving ranged attacks involves rolling a 1d10 and adding the attacker's Reflex

Resolving ranged attacks involves rolling a 1d10 and adding the attacker's Reflex and relevant weapon skill. This is contested against the defender's determined value which depends on range, weapon, and evasion skill.
