# Aimed shots are a combat mechanic where a character can use their

Aimed shots are a combat mechanic where a character can use their full action and take a -8 penalty to target specific areas like a head or leg. Successfully hitting these targets provides additional bonuses such as doubled damage through head armor or causing a Critical Injury if hitting the legs.
