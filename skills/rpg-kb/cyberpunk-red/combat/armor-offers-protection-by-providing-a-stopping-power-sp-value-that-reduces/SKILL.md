# Armor offers protection by providing a Stopping Power (SP) value that reduces

Armor offers protection by providing a Stopping Power (SP) value that reduces damage taken from attacks. Any armor worn reduces character's attributes such as REF, DEX, and MOVE by the most significant Armor Penalty of armor worn, which can impede movement and actions. The armor's SP is ablated, reducing it by 1, whenever the character takes damage. Armor doesn't stack, and only the highest SP source determines the protection level.
