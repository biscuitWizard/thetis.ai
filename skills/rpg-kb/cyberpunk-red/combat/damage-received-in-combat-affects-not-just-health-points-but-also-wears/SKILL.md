# Damage received in combat affects not just health points but also wears

Damage received in combat affects not just health points but also wears down armor, requiring repairs to restore its effectiveness. Damage is determined by subtracting armor's SP from the attack, with leftover damage reducing hit points and abating the armor's SP by one each hit until repaired.
