# Poisons and drugs require a Resist Torture/Drugs Check

Poisons and drugs require a Resist Torture/Drugs Check. Failure results in direct damage to HP or other effects, varying by intensity (mild, strong, deadly).

Source path for review: Cyberpunk RED Core Book > Combat > Critical Injuries to the Body [summary; loc 421515].
