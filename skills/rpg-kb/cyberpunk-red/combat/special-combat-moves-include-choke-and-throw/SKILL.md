# Special combat moves include Choke and Throw

Special combat moves include Choke and Throw. Choke uses a grapple to deal direct damage to hit points, potentially rendering the target unconscious, ignoring any armor. Throw ends a grapple, dealing direct damage and leaving the target prone.
