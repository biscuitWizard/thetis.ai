# Critical injuries cause additional damage and debilitating effects, with unique

Critical injuries cause additional damage and debilitating effects, with unique conditions for head and body injuries. They occur when multiple attack dice result in sixes, applying effects like dismemberment or organ damage, and can increase the death save penalty, requiring specific treatment to heal.
