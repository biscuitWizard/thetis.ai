# Cover materials in gameplay are assigned Hit Points (HP) based on their

Cover materials in gameplay are assigned Hit Points (HP) based on their type and thickness, which determine how much damage they can absorb before being destroyed. Thick and thin variants exist for different material types such as steel, stone, bulletproof glass, concrete, wood, and plaster, with thick coverings providing more protection but being less portable without high BODY stats or special equipment.
