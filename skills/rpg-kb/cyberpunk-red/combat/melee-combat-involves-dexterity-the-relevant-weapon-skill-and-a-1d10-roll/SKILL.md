# Melee combat involves Dexterity, the relevant weapon skill, and a 1d10 roll

Melee combat involves Dexterity, the relevant weapon skill, and a 1d10 roll opposed by the defender's Dexterity and evasion skill. Only very heavy weapons have a different rate of fire constraint.

Source path for review: Cyberpunk RED Core Book > Combat > Armor Breaking Combination [summary; loc 400117].
