# In Cyberpunk RED, characters have a variety of actions they can perform

In Cyberpunk RED, characters have a variety of actions they can perform during combat. Each turn, a character can move a distance equal to twice their MOVE stat. They also have multiple action options such as attacking, choking a grabbed opponent, reloading a weapon, using skills, or performing vehicle maneuvers.
