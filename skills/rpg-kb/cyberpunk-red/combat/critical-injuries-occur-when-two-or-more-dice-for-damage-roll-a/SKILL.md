# Critical Injuries occur when two or more dice for damage roll a

Critical Injuries occur when two or more dice for damage roll a 6, causing additional effects and guaranteed damage to the target's HP, bypassing armor SP. Critical Injuries inflict serious negative conditions that require specified medical interventions to heal, like surgery or paramedic intervention, with varying degrees of difficulty indicated by Difficulty Values (DV).
