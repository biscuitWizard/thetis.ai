# Drowning causes damage equal to a character's BODY stat to their HP

Drowning causes damage equal to a character's BODY stat to their HP. If asphyxiating in space, additional damage affects INT, REF, and DEX stats due to vacuum exposure, and INT reaching zero results in death.

Source path for review: Cyberpunk RED Core Book > Combat > Critical Injuries to the Body [summary; loc 421515].
