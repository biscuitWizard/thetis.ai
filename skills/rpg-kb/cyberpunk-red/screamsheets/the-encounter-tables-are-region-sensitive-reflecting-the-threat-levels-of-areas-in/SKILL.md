# The encounter tables are region-sensitive, reflecting the threat levels of areas in

The encounter tables are region-sensitive, reflecting the threat levels of areas in Night City. Moderate Threat Zones can have any kind of encounter, while Corporate Threat Zones feature fewer violent incidents but rapid corporate police response to combat. Combat and Hot Threat Zones are prone to violence and lawlessness.
