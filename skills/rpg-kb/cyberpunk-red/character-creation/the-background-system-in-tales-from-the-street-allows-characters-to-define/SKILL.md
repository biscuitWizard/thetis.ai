# The background system in "Tales from the Street" allows characters to define

The background system in "Tales from the Street" allows characters to define their origins through a roll of a die or by choice. The options range from affluent settings like Corporate Execs to harsh environments like Urban Homeless or Ganger 'Family'. This diverse array of backgrounds adds depth to character creation by influencing their early life experiences and social standing.
