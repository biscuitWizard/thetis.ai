# The creation of a complete Cyberpunk character involves multiple steps

The creation of a complete Cyberpunk character involves multiple steps. One begins by selecting a role, setting the character's Role Ability to level 4, and using both a general and role-specific Lifepath to enhance the character's narrative background. The Lifepath serves as a guide to flesh out the character’s story and worldviews, rooted deeply in Cyberpunk’s richly multicultural and multinational world.
