# Role-Based Lifepaths provide additional context and options based on a character's

Role-Based Lifepaths provide additional context and options based on a character's specific role, enriching their backstory. For example, the Rockerboy lifepath includes various career paths, enemies, performance venues, and reasons for leaving a group, while Solo lifepaths focus on the type of mercenary work, moral compass, and operational territory.
