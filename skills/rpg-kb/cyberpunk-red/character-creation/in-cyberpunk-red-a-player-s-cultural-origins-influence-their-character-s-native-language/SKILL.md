# In Cyberpunk RED, a player's cultural origins influence their character's native language

In Cyberpunk RED, a player's cultural origins influence their character's native language and cultural identity. The game assumes a common language called 'Streetslang,' but characters also know a language from their cultural region. Players roll a d10 or choose a cultural region to determine their language, starting with 4 points in that language skill. A list of common languages for each region is provided, but players can select other languages if desired.
