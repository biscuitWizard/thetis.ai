# Cyberpunk RED offers three character creation methods: Streetrat, Edgerunners, and

Cyberpunk RED offers three character creation methods: Streetrat, Edgerunners, and Complete Package. Streetrats use templates, ideal for beginners or faster play, allowing further customization through roleplaying. Edgerunners provide a flexible approach for designing a character rapidly with personalized touches. The Complete Package, the most intricate method, lets players meticulously craft their character's abilities and inventory, offering deep customization at the expense of complexity. Each approach serves different player needs, from quick character launches to detailed, customizable builds.
