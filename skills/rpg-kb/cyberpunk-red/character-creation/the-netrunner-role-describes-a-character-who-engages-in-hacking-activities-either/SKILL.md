# The Netrunner role describes a character who engages in hacking activities, either

The Netrunner role describes a character who engages in hacking activities, either as a freelancer, corporate runner, or hacktivist. Netrunners might work alone or with partners and operate out of diverse workspaces, like cluttered rooms or clean setups. They gather programs through various means such as digging in abandoned zones or purchasing them from night markets, and they might be pursued by threats like rogue AIs or rival netrunners.
