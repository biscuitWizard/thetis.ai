# Rockerboys are street poets, social conscience, and rebels in the Time of

Rockerboys are street poets, social conscience, and rebels in the Time of the Red. They challenge authority using music, poetry, art, or simple presence. The Role Ability of a Rockerboy is Charismatic Impact, allowing them to influence larger groups by sheer personality, whether they are musicians or leaders.
