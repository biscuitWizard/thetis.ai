# The workspace for characters like Netrunners or Techs doesn't count as housing

The workspace for characters like Netrunners or Techs doesn't count as housing. It's set up wherever it's possible, like in rented housing or a partner's place. If they sleep in their workspace, it’s considered as if they slept in a vehicle, which could impact their ability to perform due to lack of adequate rest.
