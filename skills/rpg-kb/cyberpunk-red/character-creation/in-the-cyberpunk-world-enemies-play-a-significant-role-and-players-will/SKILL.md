# In the Cyberpunk world, enemies play a significant role, and players will

In the Cyberpunk world, enemies play a significant role, and players will inevitably clash with them. To determine the number of enemies a character has, players roll a die and consult a table that lists different types of enemies and their motivations. These enemies could range from ex-friends to corporate executives, with varying degrees of resources they can use against the player, from just themselves to entire corporations or gangs.
