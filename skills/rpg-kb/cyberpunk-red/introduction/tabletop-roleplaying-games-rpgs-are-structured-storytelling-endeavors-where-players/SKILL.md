# Tabletop roleplaying games (RPGs) are structured storytelling endeavors where players

Tabletop roleplaying games (RPGs) are structured storytelling endeavors where players assume roles and verbally act out scenarios. Games usually involve a Gamemaster who sets the scenes and other players who navigate through the narrative and challenges together.
