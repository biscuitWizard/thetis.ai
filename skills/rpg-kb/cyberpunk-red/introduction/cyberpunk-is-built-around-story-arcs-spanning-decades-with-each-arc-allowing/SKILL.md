# Cyberpunk is built around story arcs spanning decades, with each arc allowing

Cyberpunk is built around story arcs spanning decades, with each arc allowing the world to evolve while retaining its core elements. The narrative started with the "Never Fade Away Arc" in 2013, focusing on Johnny Silverhand's assault on Arasaka Tower. The timeline then progresses to "Cyberpunk 2020" with the "Fall of the Towers Arc," culminating in significant world disruptions by 2023.
