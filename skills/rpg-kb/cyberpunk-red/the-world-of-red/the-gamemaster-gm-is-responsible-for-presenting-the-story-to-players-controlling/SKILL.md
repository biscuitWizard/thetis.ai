# The Gamemaster (GM) is responsible for presenting the story to players, controlling

The Gamemaster (GM) is responsible for presenting the story to players, controlling Non-Player Characters (NPCs), and applying and arbitrating the rules in a game. The GM creates plots where players' characters interact and make decisions, thus collaboratively crafting a story.
