# Humanity is directly linked to the EMP STAT and represents a character's

Humanity is directly linked to the EMP STAT and represents a character's ability to interact socially. For every EMP point a character has, they receive 10 points of Humanity. This is important for maintaining mental stability and avoiding cyberpsychosis, which can occur if Humanity drops too low due to trauma or cybernetic enhancements.
