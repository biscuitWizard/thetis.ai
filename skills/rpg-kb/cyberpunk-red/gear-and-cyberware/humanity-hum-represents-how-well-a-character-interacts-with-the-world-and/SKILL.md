# Humanity (HUM) represents how well a character interacts with the world and

Humanity (HUM) represents how well a character interacts with the world and the people in it. It is primarily affected by the character’s Empathy (EMP), with each point of EMP providing 10 points of Humanity. If Humanity drops below zero, the character can enter a state of cyberpsychosis, often triggered by cyberware installation or traumatic events.
