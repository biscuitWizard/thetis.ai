# The Complete Package (Calculated) Skills method gives players nearly complete control over

The Complete Package (Calculated) Skills method gives players nearly complete control over their Skills with the exception of Basic Skills all characters must have. Players also receive 86 Skill points, which can be allocated to raise Skills, following the same stipulations on Skill levels as the Edgerunner method. Unique to this method is the ability to distribute points into any Skills they choose, beyond the required Basic Skills which must be at least Level 2. Players also receive 4 free Levels in a Language of their choice based on Cultural Origin.
