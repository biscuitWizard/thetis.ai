# Complete Package Characters in Cyberpunk have much greater freedom in choosing their

Complete Package Characters in Cyberpunk have much greater freedom in choosing their weapons, armor, and gear. Unlike the more restricted templates of Edgerunners and Streetrats, these characters start with a budget of 2,550 Eurobucks to purchase items listed in the Night Market section of the game, allowing them to customize their equipment based on their needs. The intention is for characters to plan their purchases carefully since any unspent amount is kept by the character, allowing them flexibility for future advancements or changes in gameplay strategy.
