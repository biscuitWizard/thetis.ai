# Particular skills like First Aid and Paramedic enable characters to treat injuries

Particular skills like First Aid and Paramedic enable characters to treat injuries and prevent fatal outcomes. First Aid deals with common injuries, while Paramedic (x2) deals with more severe cases, although surgery remains exclusive to Medtechs through their Role Ability.
