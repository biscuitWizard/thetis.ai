# Skills in Cyberpunk RED are abilities and knowledge that a character has

Skills in Cyberpunk RED are abilities and knowledge that a character has built through training or experience. They are linked to STATs representing natural ability and are categorized into nine types: Awareness, Body, Control, Education, Fighting, Performance, Ranged Weapon, Social, and Technique Skills.
