# Cyberpsychosis is a critical danger for those who overuse cybernetic enhancements,

Cyberpsychosis is a critical danger for those who overuse cybernetic enhancements, especially if they already have psychopathic tendencies. This disorder leads individuals to lose their humanity, manifesting as a lack of self-preservation and disregard for others. Edgerunners frequently face situations that threaten their mental state, prompting potential humanity loss, a concept controlled by the game master (GM). Additional cyberware contributes to this disorder when it replaces or enhances beyond baseline human abilities, unless it serves a medical or therapeutic purpose, like gender affirmation surgeries which carry no humanity cost.
