# Exotic Weapons in the Cyberpunk universe are highly specialized or unique variants

Exotic Weapons in the Cyberpunk universe are highly specialized or unique variants of regular weapon types. They cannot use standard weapon attachments or non-basic ammunition unless specified. Exotic Weapons have an average quality level and are expensive and rare, often requiring assistance from a Fixer to be found in the Night Market. Carrying one can make a person stand out, greatly impacting social interactions.
