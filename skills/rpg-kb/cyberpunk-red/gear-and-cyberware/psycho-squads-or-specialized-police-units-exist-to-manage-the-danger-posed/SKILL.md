# Psycho Squads, or specialized police units, exist to manage the danger posed

Psycho Squads, or specialized police units, exist to manage the danger posed by cyberpsychos. These squads are well-armed and tasked with capturing or neutralizing cybernetic individuals who become uncontrollable. In the absence of a centralized government, local militias may form to assume these roles, highlighting the societal hazards of cybernetic enhancements.
