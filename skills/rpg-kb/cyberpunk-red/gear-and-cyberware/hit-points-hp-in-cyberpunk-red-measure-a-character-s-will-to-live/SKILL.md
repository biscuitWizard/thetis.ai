# Hit Points (HP) in Cyberpunk RED measure a character's will to live

Hit Points (HP) in Cyberpunk RED measure a character's will to live and their overall physical condition. Damage taken by the character is subtracted from their HP, with penalties applied as HP reaches certain thresholds. The Seriously Wounded Threshold is half of the total HP (rounded up), and the Death Save is determined by the BODY statistic.
