# Cyberware, the key technological enhancement in the Cyberpunk universe, comes in eight

Cyberware, the key technological enhancement in the Cyberpunk universe, comes in eight types: Fashionware, Neuralware, Cyberoptics, Cyberaudio, Internal and External Body Cyberware, Cyberlimbs, and Borgware. Each type has specific functions and installation requirements, providing various enhancements but also posing the risk of humanity loss. Humanity Loss is a major consideration when installing cyberware, affecting the character's psychological state, except when the cyberware is for therapeutic purposes.
