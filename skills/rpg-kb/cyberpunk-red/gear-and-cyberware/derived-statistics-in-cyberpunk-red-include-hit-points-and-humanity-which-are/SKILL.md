# Derived Statistics in Cyberpunk RED include Hit Points and Humanity, which are

Derived Statistics in Cyberpunk RED include Hit Points and Humanity, which are calculated from primary STATS like BODY and WILL. Hit Points indicate a character's physical state and will to live, and are crucial during combat situations. Humanity, derived from the Empathy STAT, affects how a character interacts with others and may impact their mental state. Low Humanity can lead to cyberpsychosis, especially when installing large amounts of cyberware.
