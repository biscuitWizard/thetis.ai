# Combat mechanics in the Cyberpunk world are defined by the Initiative Queue,

Combat mechanics in the Cyberpunk world are defined by the Initiative Queue, decided by initiative rolls, and proceeding in order from highest to lowest. Each entity takes a turn involving one Move Action and one regular Action, participating in a structured combat rhythm that repeats until resolution.
