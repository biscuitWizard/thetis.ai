# Characters may find themselves involuntarily embedded with sabotage chips that enforce

Characters may find themselves involuntarily embedded with sabotage chips that enforce compliance or even trigger violent actions against their will, such as committing murder upon a command. Such chips can also inflict pain or cause heart stoppage if the character attempts to harm the Corporation.
