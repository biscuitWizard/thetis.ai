# To ensure control over their operatives, Corporations employ a variety of coercive

To ensure control over their operatives, Corporations employ a variety of coercive tactics which include taking hostages, blackmail, and sabotaging cybernetics. This gives them the ability to monitor, track, or directly manipulate these individuals, often under threat of harm to their loved ones or themselves.
