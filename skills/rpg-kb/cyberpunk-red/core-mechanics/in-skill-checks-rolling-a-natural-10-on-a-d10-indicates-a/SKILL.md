# In skill checks, rolling a natural 10 on a d10 indicates a

In skill checks, rolling a natural 10 on a d10 indicates a Critical Success, prompting an additional roll, while a natural 1 prompts a Critical Failure, incurring a penalty by reducing the initial roll. Modifiers can affect the Difficulty Value in various conditions.
