# In combat, a 'Hold Action' allows a character to delay one specific

In combat, a 'Hold Action' allows a character to delay one specific action until a designated point in the initiative queue or until a certain condition is met. This action cannot be carried over multiple rounds, and the triggering condition or timing cannot be altered once set. Move actions and NET actions cannot be held in this way.
