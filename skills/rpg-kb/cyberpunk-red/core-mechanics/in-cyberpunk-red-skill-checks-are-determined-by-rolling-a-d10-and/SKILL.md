# In Cyberpunk RED, Skill Checks are determined by rolling a d10 and

In Cyberpunk RED, Skill Checks are determined by rolling a d10 and adding a STAT and a Skill to reach or exceed a Difficulty Value (DV). Two specific terms are important here: Critical Success and Critical Failure. A Critical Success occurs when a player rolls a natural 10 on the d10, allowing them to roll another d10 and add it to their initial roll. A Critical Failure happens on a natural roll of 1, prompting another roll of a d10, with the result being subtracted from the total value of STAT, Skill, and the first roll.
