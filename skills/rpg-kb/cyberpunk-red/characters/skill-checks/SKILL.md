# Cyberpunk RED skill checks

For an uncertain task, choose the STAT and Skill that fit the attempted approach and combine them with the game's random roll. Compare the result with an opponent's result or a difficulty selected for the task. Equipment, time, injury, environment, and special abilities may modify the attempt; consult the licensed rules for their exact treatment.
