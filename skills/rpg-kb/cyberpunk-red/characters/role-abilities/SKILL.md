# Cyberpunk RED role abilities

A Role identifies a professional niche and provides a signature ability beyond ordinary STAT and Skill ratings. That ability creates distinctive leverage in play, such as access, coordination, technical control, reputation, or institutional influence. Each Role follows its own procedure and advancement details, which this skeleton intentionally does not reproduce.
