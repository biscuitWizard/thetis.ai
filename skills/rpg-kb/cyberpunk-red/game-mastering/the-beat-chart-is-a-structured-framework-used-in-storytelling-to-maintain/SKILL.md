# The Beat Chart is a structured framework used in storytelling to maintain

The Beat Chart is a structured framework used in storytelling to maintain dramatic tension and make the storyline coherent. It consists of five aspects, or Beats: the Hook, Development, Cliffhanger, Climax, and Resolution. The Hook, Climax, and Resolution occur only once, while Development and Cliffhanger alternate throughout the story.
