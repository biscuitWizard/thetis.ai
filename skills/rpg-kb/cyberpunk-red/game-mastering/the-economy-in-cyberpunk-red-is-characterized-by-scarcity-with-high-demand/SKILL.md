# The economy in Cyberpunk RED is characterized by scarcity, with high demand

The economy in Cyberpunk RED is characterized by scarcity, with high demand and low supply. Transactions are influenced by Market Price categories, which define the cost range from 'Cheap' to 'Super Luxury.' Access to items depends on connections; Fixers and their night markets are crucial for purchasing more expensive items. The principle "Everything is worth what its purchaser will pay for it" encapsulates the fluid nature of the economy.
