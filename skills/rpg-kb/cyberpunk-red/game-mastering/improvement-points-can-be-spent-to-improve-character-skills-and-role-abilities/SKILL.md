# Improvement Points can be spent to improve character skills and role abilities

Improvement Points can be spent to improve character skills and role abilities. The cost to improve varies based on the difficulty and level/rank of the skill. Typical skills cost from 20 I.P. at level 1 to 200 I.P. at level 10, while difficult skills cost double, from 40 I.P. at level 1 to 400 I.P. at level 10.
