# Edgerunners in this world often take on hazardous jobs for a living

Edgerunners in this world often take on hazardous jobs for a living. Pay aligns with the job's danger level ranging from 500eb for easy jobs to 2,000eb for dangerous tasks, assuming each mission is resolved with success. Roles like bodyguards or muscle service also offer a range of income based on a character's skill rank and the outcome of a d6 roll during what's termed as the "hustle."
