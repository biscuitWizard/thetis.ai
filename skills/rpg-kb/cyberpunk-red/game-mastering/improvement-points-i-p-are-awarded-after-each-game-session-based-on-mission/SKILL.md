# Improvement Points (I.P.) are awarded after each game session based on mission

Improvement Points (I.P.) are awarded after each game session based on mission success and individual player contributions, especially aligning with their identified playstyles. Points reflect both group accomplishments and standout individual actions, rewarding contributions in combat, exploration, social support, and roleplaying.
