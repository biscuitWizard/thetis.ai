# The Fixer's Role Ability, Operator, is characterized by three main facets: Contacts,

The Fixer's Role Ability, Operator, is characterized by three main facets: Contacts, Reach, and Haggle. Fixers access goods and favors via a network of contacts and can influence markets to source varying categories of items through night markets. They can also use their Grease ability to navigate different cultures and societal groups.
