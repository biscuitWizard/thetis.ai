# The Medicine Role Ability belongs to Medtechs who are skilled in keeping

The Medicine Role Ability belongs to Medtechs who are skilled in keeping individuals alive largely via mechanical and technical means. With each rank increase, Medtechs can allocate points to Surgery, Medical Tech (Pharmaceuticals), or Medical Tech (Cryosystem Operation), unlocking various medical capabilities and skills.
