# The Exec role in mechanics focuses on building and managing a team

The Exec role in mechanics focuses on building and managing a team to achieve objectives, either legally or otherwise. The Teamwork ability allows Execs to recruit team members and utilize company resources, like corporate housing and health insurance, as their rank increases.
