# The Acting skill measures the ability to disguise oneself and assume roles

The Acting skill measures the ability to disguise oneself and assume roles. At Base 10, solid disguises are achievable, fooling most people. Base 14 allows for advanced impersonation, including mimicking voices and altering appearances. At Base 18, the skill is so effective that one can convincingly take over an individual's life.
