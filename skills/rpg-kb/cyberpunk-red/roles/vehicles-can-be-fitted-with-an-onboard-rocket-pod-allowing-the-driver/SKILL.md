# Vehicles can be fitted with an Onboard Rocket Pod, allowing the driver

Vehicles can be fitted with an Onboard Rocket Pod, allowing the driver to fire rockets through an integrated launcher. This upgrade requires a Heavy Chassis and offers significant offensive capabilities but cannot be reloaded on the move.
