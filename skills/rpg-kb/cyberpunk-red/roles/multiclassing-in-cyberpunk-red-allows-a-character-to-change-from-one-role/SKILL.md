# Multiclassing in Cyberpunk RED allows a character to change from one Role

Multiclassing in Cyberpunk RED allows a character to change from one Role to another after achieving at least 4 Ranks in their current Role. Characters retain abilities from their previous Role but start the new Role at Rank 1. Additional multiclassing is restricted until the new Role reaches Rank 4.
