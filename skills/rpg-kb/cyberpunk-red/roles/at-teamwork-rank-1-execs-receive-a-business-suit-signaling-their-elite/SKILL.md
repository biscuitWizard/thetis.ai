# At Teamwork Rank 1, Execs receive a business suit signaling their elite

At Teamwork Rank 1, Execs receive a business suit signaling their elite status, but cannot sell it without suspicion. By Rank 2, they obtain access to corporate apartments without rent, which transforms into a house or penthouse at higher ranks.
