# The Rockerboy's Role Ability is Charismatic Impact, allowing them to exert influence

The Rockerboy's Role Ability is Charismatic Impact, allowing them to exert influence through personality rather than just performance. Success depends on the size of the group and the presence of fans. The ability's effectiveness grows with higher Ranks, expanding their influence from local clubs at lower Ranks to potentially leading a cult-like global following at Rank 10.
