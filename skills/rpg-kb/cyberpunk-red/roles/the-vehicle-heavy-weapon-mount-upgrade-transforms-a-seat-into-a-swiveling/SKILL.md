# The Vehicle Heavy Weapon Mount upgrade transforms a seat into a swiveling

The Vehicle Heavy Weapon Mount upgrade transforms a seat into a swiveling mount for two-handed weapons, which a passenger can operate even while in motion. It includes additional protection if the vehicle has bulletproof glass and requires a Heavy Chassis.
