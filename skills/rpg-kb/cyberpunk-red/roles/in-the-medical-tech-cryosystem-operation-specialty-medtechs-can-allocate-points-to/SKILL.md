# In the Medical Tech (Cryosystem Operation) specialty, Medtechs can allocate points to

In the Medical Tech (Cryosystem Operation) specialty, Medtechs can allocate points to gain abilities related to Cryosystems. This skill is exclusive to Medtechs and allows them to operate, understand, and repair medical machinery. Points allocated enhance Cryotank and Cryopump capabilities, such as increasing the number of Cryotanks and the capacity to carry people in stasis.
