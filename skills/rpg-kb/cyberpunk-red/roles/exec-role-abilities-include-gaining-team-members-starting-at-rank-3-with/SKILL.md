# Exec role abilities include gaining team members starting at rank 3, with

Exec role abilities include gaining team members starting at rank 3, with additional team members at higher ranks. Team Members have unique stats and follow orders based on loyalty, which shifts according to the Exec's actions. Loyalty determines how effectively they follow orders and can affect their willingness to continue working with the Exec.
