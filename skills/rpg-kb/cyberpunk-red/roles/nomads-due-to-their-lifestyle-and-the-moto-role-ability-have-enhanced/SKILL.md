# Nomads, due to their lifestyle and the Moto Role Ability, have enhanced

Nomads, due to their lifestyle and the Moto Role Ability, have enhanced vehicle knowledge and skills, adding their Moto Rank to multiple vehicle-related skill checks. As they upgrade their Role Ability Rank, they can select either a new stock vehicle or an upgrade for one of their existing family vehicles.
