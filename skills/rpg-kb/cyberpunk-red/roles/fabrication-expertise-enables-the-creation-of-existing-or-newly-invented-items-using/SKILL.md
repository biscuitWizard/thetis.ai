# Fabrication Expertise enables the creation of existing or newly invented items using

Fabrication Expertise enables the creation of existing or newly invented items using materials corresponding to their cost. Like upgrading, this involves a TECH roll and is subject to difficulty and time constraints based on the item's price. Failures necessitate restarting the process, retaining materials.
