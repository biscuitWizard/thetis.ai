# The Smuggling Upgrade equips vehicles with hidden holsters for concealed weapons and

The Smuggling Upgrade equips vehicles with hidden holsters for concealed weapons and a large hidden space for smuggling. These compartments are difficult to discover, requiring a specific skill check. This upgrade can be installed on most vehicles, except for bikes, jetskis, and gyrocopters.
