# When team members lose loyalty or are lost, HR replaces them with

When team members lose loyalty or are lost, HR replaces them with new members who start with reduced loyalty and require a hiring fee. Team member loyalty is measured by a Loyalty Save, which, if failed, may result in refusal or betrayal of tasks.
