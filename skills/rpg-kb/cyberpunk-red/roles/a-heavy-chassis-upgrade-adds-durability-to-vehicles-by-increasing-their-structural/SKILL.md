# A Heavy Chassis upgrade adds durability to vehicles by increasing their Structural

A Heavy Chassis upgrade adds durability to vehicles by increasing their Structural Damage Points (SDP) and enabling them to tow heavy loads. This upgrade is essential for certain advanced modifications, like adding rocket pods or weapon mounts.
