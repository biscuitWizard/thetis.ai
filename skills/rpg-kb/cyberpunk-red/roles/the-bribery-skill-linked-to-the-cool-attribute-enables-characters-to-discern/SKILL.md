# The Bribery skill, linked to the COOL attribute, enables characters to discern

The Bribery skill, linked to the COOL attribute, enables characters to discern when and how to bribe effectively. At Base 10, they can secure simple favors; at Base 18, they can sway even the most incorruptible individuals.
