# Nomad upgrades include a variety of enhancements for vehicles that improve functionality

Nomad upgrades include a variety of enhancements for vehicles that improve functionality and defense, such as Armored Chassis, Bulletproof Glass, and NOS systems. Some upgrades, like the Hover Upgrade, even allow vehicles to travel over water, while others, like the AV-4 Engine Upgrade, enable flight.
