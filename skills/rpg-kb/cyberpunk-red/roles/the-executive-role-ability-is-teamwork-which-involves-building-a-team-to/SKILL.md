# The Executive Role Ability is 'Teamwork', which involves building a team to

The Executive Role Ability is 'Teamwork', which involves building a team to assist in achieving objectives. This role includes benefits like gaining corporate attire, housing, and health insurance, with increased perks as the Exec's Teamwork rank rises. At higher ranks, Execs can influence important corporate or political figures and leverage their position to gain additional resources.
