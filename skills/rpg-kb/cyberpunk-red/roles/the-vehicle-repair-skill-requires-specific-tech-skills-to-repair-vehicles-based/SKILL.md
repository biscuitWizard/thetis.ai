# The Vehicle Repair skill requires specific TECH Skills to repair vehicles based

The Vehicle Repair skill requires specific TECH Skills to repair vehicles based on the damage type: Minor Damage requires DV9 and 3 hours, Major Damage needs DV13 and a day, and Destroyed vehicles require DV17 and a week. Failure results in having to restart the repair process.

Source path for review: Cyberpunk RED Core Book > Roles > Charismatic Impact Ranks 1 and 2 [summary; loc 314534].
