# Loyalty of team members is crucial; it determines how they follow orders

Loyalty of team members is crucial; it determines how they follow orders. Execs must actively promote loyalty through compliments, bonuses, or risk physical harm on their behalf, but berate or ignore them at their peril as it reduces loyalty.
