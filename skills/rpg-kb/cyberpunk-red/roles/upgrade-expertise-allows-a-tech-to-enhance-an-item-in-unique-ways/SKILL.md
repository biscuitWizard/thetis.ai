# Upgrade Expertise allows a Tech to enhance an item in unique ways,

Upgrade Expertise allows a Tech to enhance an item in unique ways, such as reducing humanity loss for cyberware, increasing quality, or adding slots for attachments. The process requires a TECH roll combined with relevant skills and resources, with difficulty and time based on the item’s cost. Failures mean starting over without damage to the item or loss of materials.
