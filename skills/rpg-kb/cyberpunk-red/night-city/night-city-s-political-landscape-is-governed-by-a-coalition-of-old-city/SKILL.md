# Night City's political landscape is governed by a coalition of old city

Night City's political landscape is governed by a coalition of old city government, Edgerunners, Nomad factions, and influential Corporations like Biotechnica and Militech. Each district is managed by its own City Manager, while the concept of a centralized government remains weak due to ongoing factional disputes. Despite instability, the city gradually functions without descending into chaos.
