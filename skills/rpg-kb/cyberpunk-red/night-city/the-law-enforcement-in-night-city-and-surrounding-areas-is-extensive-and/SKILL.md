# The law enforcement in Night City and surrounding areas is extensive and

The law enforcement in Night City and surrounding areas is extensive and diverse, with Lawmen handling city policing, and the Cyberpsycho Squad tackling cybernetic criminals. Corporate Cops heavily guard corporate facilities, often showing more aggression due to their powerful backing. The legal framework in Night City stems from the Uniform Civilian Justice Code, a remnant from when martial law replaced civilian laws with military codes, emphasizing strict crime control. Despite strict laws, socio-economic disparities lead to uneven enforcement depending on wealth and location.
