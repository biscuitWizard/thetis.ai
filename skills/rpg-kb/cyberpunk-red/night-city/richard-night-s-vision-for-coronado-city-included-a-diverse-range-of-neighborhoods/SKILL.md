# Richard Night's vision for Coronado City included a diverse range of neighborhoods,

Richard Night's vision for Coronado City included a diverse range of neighborhoods, each reflecting different cultural styles, and was designed to be a model community integrating varied architectural themes. This included districts with faux east coast styles, western styles, comfortable urban living spaces, an entertainment-focused Marina, and culturally diverse Asian districts. Night even incorporated elements from the original Morro Bay to preserve its historical essence.
