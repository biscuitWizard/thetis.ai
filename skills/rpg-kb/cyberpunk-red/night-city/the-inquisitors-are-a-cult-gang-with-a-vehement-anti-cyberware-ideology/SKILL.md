# The Inquisitors are a cult gang with a vehement anti-cyberware ideology

The Inquisitors are a cult gang with a vehement anti-cyberware ideology. They violently oppose cybernetic enhancements and consider removing them a form of soul-saving. This gang is universally despised by other gangs across the city.
