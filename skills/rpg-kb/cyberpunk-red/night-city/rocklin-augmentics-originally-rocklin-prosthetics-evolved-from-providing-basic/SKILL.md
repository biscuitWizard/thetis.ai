# Rocklin Augmentics, originally Rocklin Prosthetics, evolved from providing basic

Despite facing controversies regarding potential illegal AI experiments, Rocklin maintains a strong domestic reputation as "American first," issuing strong responses to accusations and legal actions against dissenters. Hidalgo is known for her bold leadership and significant personal augmentations, continually pushing the company forward in the cybernetics field.
