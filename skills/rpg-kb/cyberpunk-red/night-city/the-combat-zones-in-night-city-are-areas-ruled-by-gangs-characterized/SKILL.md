# The Combat Zones in Night City are areas ruled by gangs, characterized

The Combat Zones in Night City are areas ruled by gangs, characterized by slums, tenements, and ruins of abandoned city blocks. Gangs control resources and aggressively protect their turf. Occasionally, Corporate or City forces conduct violent raids to eliminate gang influence. Notable neighborhoods in these zones include Old Japantown, Little China, Old Combat Zone, and South Night City.
