# Night City became nearly uninhabitable post-detonation, leading to fires and destruction

Night City became nearly uninhabitable post-detonation, leading to fires and destruction of housing. Residents were forced to relocate to suburbs, and neighborhoods began seeking food rather than rebuilding the city.
