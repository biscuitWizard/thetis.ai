# Rocklin Augmentics has grown significantly since its transformation, establishing new

Rocklin Augmentics has grown significantly since its transformation, establishing new facilities across several U.S. and Free State cities. Despite their considerable influence in the domestic augmentations field, focusing on cyberlimb and skeletal enhancements, Rocklin maintains a commitment to being an "American company." The company also employs many individuals displaced during the Time of the Red.
