# Richard Night, born in Pasadena, California, was a visionary entrepreneur who sought

Richard Night, born in Pasadena, California, was a visionary entrepreneur who sought to build a futuristic city immune to the societal collapse of his time. He founded Halsey, Ferris, and Night, a successful development company known for constructing extensive projects globally. Inspired by his background in engineering and business, Night aimed to create Coronado City, later known as Night City, as a self-sufficient, planned urban environment capable of resisting outside threats.
