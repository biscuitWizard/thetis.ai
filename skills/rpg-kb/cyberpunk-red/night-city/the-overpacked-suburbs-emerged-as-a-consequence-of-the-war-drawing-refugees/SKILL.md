# The Overpacked Suburbs emerged as a consequence of the War, drawing refugees

The Overpacked Suburbs emerged as a consequence of the War, drawing refugees into tent cities and camps due to the central city's lack of basic services. While these suburbs were previously safe and residential, they are now crowded and crime-ridden. Key areas within these suburbs include Heywood and Rancho Coronado.
