# Mister Kernaghan, a former IT worker for the Night City Port Authority,

Mister Kernaghan, a former IT worker for the Night City Port Authority, built his empire following the 4th Corporate War by seizing shipping data and using it to trade goods initially essential items and later luxury goods in Night City's recovering market. He's known for holding Midnight Markets for the elite, where items like Aeropozes and Cryo-Max suits are sold.
