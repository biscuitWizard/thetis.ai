# Jacinda Hidalgo, CEO of Rocklin Augmentics, lost her legs to a landmine

Jacinda Hidalgo, CEO of Rocklin Augmentics, lost her legs to a landmine at age 10. Adopted by Andrew Rocklin, she took over the company at 26 after his mysterious disappearance. Known for her innovation and artistry, Jacinda has greatly contributed to Rocklin's success in the competitive cybernetics market. She personally displays the company's products, showcasing impressive agility with her own extensive augmentations.
