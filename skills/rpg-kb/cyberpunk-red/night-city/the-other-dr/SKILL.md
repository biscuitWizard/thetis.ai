# The Other Dr

The Other Dr. Bob, a medtech skilled in cybernetic implants, refuses to perform facial reconstructions for poser gangs due to a personal vendetta against them after losing his hands to them. Often mistaken for another doctor, he remains a notable figure for his connections with boostergangs in Night City.
