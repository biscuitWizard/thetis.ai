# Night City is made up of various Zones with different Threat Ratings:

Night City is made up of various Zones with different Threat Ratings: Executive, Corporate, Moderate, Combat, and Outskirts. Each rating dictates the levels of security and danger inhabitants may face, with the Hot Zone typically ranked highest in danger.
