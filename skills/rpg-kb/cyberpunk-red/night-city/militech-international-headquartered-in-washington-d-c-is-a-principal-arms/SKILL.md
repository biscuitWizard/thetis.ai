# Militech International, headquartered in Washington D.C., is a principal arms

Militech International, headquartered in Washington D.C., is a principal arms manufacturer and mercenary service provider. Despite being partially nationalized, they continue to be a dominant force with secret rebuilding efforts to reclaim their lost power. Militech was heavily involved in the 4th Corporate War, clashing with Arasaka Corp and subsequently losing significant resources. Under the leadership of Gen. Donald Lundee, they retain worldwide influence and supply the New United States extensively while engaging globally with various clients willing to pay for their mercenary services and advanced weaponry.
