# David Ling Po, a seemingly upright businessman, secretly leads the Weng Fang

David Ling Po, a seemingly upright businessman, secretly leads the Weng Fang Tong, a powerful Chinese criminal syndicate in Night City. He oversees various illegal activities through networks of boostergangs and extensive surveillance. Meanwhile, Dr. Edward Michaels, a well-respected academic leader, is committed to maintaining equality and independence from corporate control at Night City University.
