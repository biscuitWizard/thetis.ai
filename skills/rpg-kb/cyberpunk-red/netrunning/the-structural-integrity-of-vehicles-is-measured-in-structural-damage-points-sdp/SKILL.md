# The structural integrity of vehicles is measured in Structural Damage Points (SDP)

The structural integrity of vehicles is measured in Structural Damage Points (SDP). A vehicle can still move as long as it retains at least one SDP, and dodging in vehicles is akin to dodging regular attacks. Targeting specific weak points can allow for more effective attacks through vehicle glass.
