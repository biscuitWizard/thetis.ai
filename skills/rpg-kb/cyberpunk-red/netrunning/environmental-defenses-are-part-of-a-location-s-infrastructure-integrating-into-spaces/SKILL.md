# Environmental Defenses are part of a location's infrastructure, integrating into spaces

Environmental Defenses are part of a location's infrastructure, integrating into spaces like rooms or corridors. They perpetually defend an area unless disarmed, using their own Control Nodes in the NET Architecture. Examples include Observation Cameras and specialized flooring systems designed to restrict or harm intruders.
