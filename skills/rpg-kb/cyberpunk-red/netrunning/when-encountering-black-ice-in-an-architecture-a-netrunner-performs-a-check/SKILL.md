# When encountering Black ICE in an Architecture, a Netrunner performs a check

When encountering Black ICE in an Architecture, a Netrunner performs a check via their Interface and any SPEED bonus against the Black ICE’s SPEED. If Black ICE wins, its effects are immediately applied and it takes a top position in the Initiative Queue, attacking on its subsequent turns.
