# Vehicles in Cyberpunk RED have Structural Damage Points that function similarly to

Vehicles in Cyberpunk RED have Structural Damage Points that function similarly to hit points; a vehicle can continue operating as long as it has at least one SDP. If a vehicle's SDP is depleted, it is considered destroyed. Characters can also perform specific actions in vehicles, like entering or exiting, starting or stopping, and these often use up their turn. Additionally, when a vehicle starts, it alters the initiative order and influences how the driver's MOVE STAT is perceived.
