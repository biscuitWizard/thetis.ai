# A Netrunner can activate their own Black ICE to lie in wait

A Netrunner can activate their own Black ICE to lie in wait at a specific 'floor' of the Architecture outside combat, or during combat targeting an enemy. Once activated, Black ICE enters the Initiative Queue and continues attacking its target each Turn until it is Derezzed or Slid away from.

Source path for review: Cyberpunk RED Core Book > Netrunning > DEMONS [summary; loc 486362].
