# Netrunners perform actions in the net called NET Actions, which are distinct

Netrunners perform actions in the net called NET Actions, which are distinct from Meat Actions that occur in the physical world. The number of NET Actions a player can perform depends on their Interface rank, a Netrunner's unique role ability. For example, a Rank 1 Netrunner can perform 2 NET Actions per turn, whereas a Rank 4 can perform 3 NET Actions.
