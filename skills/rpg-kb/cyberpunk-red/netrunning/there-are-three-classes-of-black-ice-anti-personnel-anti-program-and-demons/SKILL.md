# There are three classes of Black ICE: Anti-Personnel, Anti-Program, and Demons

There are three classes of Black ICE: Anti-Personnel, Anti-Program, and Demons. Anti-Personnel Black ICE target and kill Netrunners, Anti-Program Black ICE destroy active Programs of a Netrunner, while Demons defend NET Architectures by targeting Netrunners and their parties in both the digital and physical world.
