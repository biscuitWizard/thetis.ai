# When encountering Black ICE in an architecture, a Netrunner needs to roll

When encountering Black ICE in an architecture, a Netrunner needs to roll a check versus the Black ICE's Speed attribute. If Black ICE wins, it immediately attacks its target, which occurs continuously each turn until derezzed or eluded using a Slide ability. Netrunners can also activate their own Black ICE during combat, which will attack relentlessly until conditions change.
