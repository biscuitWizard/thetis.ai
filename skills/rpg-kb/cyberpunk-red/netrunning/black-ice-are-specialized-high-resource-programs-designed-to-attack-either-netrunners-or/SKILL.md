# Black ICE are specialized, high-resource programs designed to attack either Netrunners or

Black ICE are specialized, high-resource programs designed to attack either Netrunners or their Programs. Anti-Personnel Black ICE targets Netrunners directly, while Anti-Program Black ICE focuses on destroying Rezzed Programs. They occupy multiple slots in a Cyberdeck, thus consuming more resources than typical programs.
