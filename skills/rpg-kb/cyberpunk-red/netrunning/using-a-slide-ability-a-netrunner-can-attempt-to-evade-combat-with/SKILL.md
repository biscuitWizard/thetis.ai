# Using a Slide ability, a Netrunner can attempt to evade combat with

Using a Slide ability, a Netrunner can attempt to evade combat with Black ICE Programs by rolling against the program's perception. Success allows moving past the current obstacle, but failed attempts prevent further evasion that turn.
