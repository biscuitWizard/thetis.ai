# The Wisp is a type of Anti-Personnel Black ICE that deals 1d6

The Wisp is a type of Anti-Personnel Black ICE that deals 1d6 damage directly to an enemy Netrunner's brain and reduces the total NET Actions the Netrunner can perform on their next turn by 1, with a minimum of 2 NET Actions remaining. It is considered a Costly ICE at 50eb and is depicted as an energetic orb of light with a minuscule eye.

Source path for review: Cyberpunk RED Core Book > Netrunning > ACTIVE DEFENSES [summary; loc 494571].
