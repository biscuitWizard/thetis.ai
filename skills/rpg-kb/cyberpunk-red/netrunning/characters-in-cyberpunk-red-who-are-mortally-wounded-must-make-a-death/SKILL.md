# Characters in Cyberpunk RED who are mortally wounded must make a death

Characters in Cyberpunk RED who are mortally wounded must make a death save at the start of their turn. This involves rolling a d10 and the result has to be lower than their BODY stat for them to survive. Rolling a 10 is an automatic failure, resulting in death. Each time a death save is attempted, the character’s death save penalty increases by 1, making it progressively harder to survive without stabilization.
