# Programs within the NET can be derezzed when their REZ is reduced

Programs within the NET can be derezzed when their REZ is reduced to zero, making them inactive but not removing them. Programs that are destroyed must be repurchased. Attack programs are unique as they deactivate after use and require reactivation.

Source path for review: Cyberpunk RED Core Book > Netrunning > How to Read the Black ICE Program Table [summary; loc 471384].
