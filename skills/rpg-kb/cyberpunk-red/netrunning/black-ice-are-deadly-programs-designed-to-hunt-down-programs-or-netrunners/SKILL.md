# Black ICE are deadly Programs designed to hunt down Programs or Netrunners

Black ICE are deadly Programs designed to hunt down Programs or Netrunners within an Architecture. They require two slots in a deck and Activation or Deactivation requires a NET Action. Installation or removal of Black ICE Programs takes an hour.
