# Cyberdeck hardware offers additional functionalities by utilizing Option Slots

Cyberdeck hardware offers additional functionalities by utilizing Option Slots. Examples include the Backup Drive, which saves destroyed Non-Black ICE Programs and can reinstall them; DNA Lock, which secures Cyberdecks with biometric locks; Hardened Circuitry, making Cyberdecks immune to EMP Effects; and the KRASH Barrier, protecting against forced Jack Outs. Most of these upgrades are priced at 100eb and take up one or more Hardware Option Slots.
