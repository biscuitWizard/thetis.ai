# The Dragon, Killer, and Sabertooth are Anti-Program Black ICE that focus on

The Dragon, Killer, and Sabertooth are Anti-Program Black ICE that focus on damaging enemy programs. Dragon and Sabertooth deal 6d6 damage, while Killer deals 4d6. If any of this damage would Derezz (render inoperative) a Program, the Program is instead Destroyed. These ICE serve as formidable obstacles to Netrunner Programs, with costs of 1,000eb for Dragon and Sabertooth, and 500eb for Killer. Dragon appears as a golden-scaled dragon robot, Killer as a robotic samurai with a glowing katana, and Sabertooth as an enormous cat with glowing tusks.
