# Night City was severely impacted during the 4th Corporate War, with damage

Night City was severely impacted during the 4th Corporate War, with damage still visible twenty-two years later. Initially dominated by Militech and Arasaka forces, it underwent massive destruction culminating in a nuclear event known as The Red. This catastrophic period left a toxic environment but drove determination to rebuild with the support of the Pacifica Confederation.
