# The Pacifica Confederation, consisting of Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Northern California,

The Pacifica Confederation, consisting of Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Northern California, and British Columbia, formed post-War to rebuild infrastructure. While hosting several U.S. government bases, they operate with significant autonomy, especially rejecting oppressive control from the central U.S. government.
