# In the fallout of the DataKrash and R.A.B.I.D.s, the NET became too

In the fallout of the DataKrash and R.A.B.I.D.s, the NET became too dangerous to use, filled with corrupted data and lethal autonomous programs. This prompted Netwatch, the global cybersecurity organization, to propagate IGTA Dissolution Prime, effectively shutting down the ability of NET-compatible devices to connect to cyberspace. Thus, the NET was declared dead in March 2023, marking the end of a critical era in digital communication.
