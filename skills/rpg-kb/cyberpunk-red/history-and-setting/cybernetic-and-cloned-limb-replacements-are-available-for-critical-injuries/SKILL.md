# Cybernetic and cloned limb replacements are available for critical injuries

Cybernetic and cloned limb replacements are available for critical injuries. Medical-grade cyberware restores function without upgrades and doesn't cause Humanity loss. Alternatively, a full cybernetic limb can be purchased for future enhancements, with costs dependent on the type of replacement.
