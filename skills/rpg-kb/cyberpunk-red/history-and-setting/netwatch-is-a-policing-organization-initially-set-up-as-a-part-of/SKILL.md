# Netwatch is a policing organization initially set up as a part of

Netwatch is a policing organization initially set up as a part of a U.S./EuroTheatre treaty, primarily funded by governments and Megacorporations. Its early role involved monitoring the NET for illegal activities and managing various regions' digital territories. The organization employed expert Netrunners to protect the NET from cyber threats. However, the NET was ultimately destroyed due to the actions of a master Netrunner, Rache Bartmoss, leaving Netwatch without a network to police. In recent times, Netwatch's role has shifted to rescuing Netrunners who accidentally enter the dangerous remnants of the Old NET, filled with malicious Black ICE programs. Netwatch, with the help of Alt Cunningham and transcendental AIs, is secretly working on a project known only as the Black Wall, intended to restore the NET's functionality and security.
