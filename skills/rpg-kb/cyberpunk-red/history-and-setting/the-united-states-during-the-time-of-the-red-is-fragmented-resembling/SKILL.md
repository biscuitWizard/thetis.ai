# The United States during the Time of the Red is fragmented, resembling

The United States during the Time of the Red is fragmented, resembling a historical division akin to the late 1800s with a 'civilized' East Coast under federal control, and a 'Wild West' beyond the Mississippi River. The West Coast operates independently through local militia and mercenary armies to maintain order.
