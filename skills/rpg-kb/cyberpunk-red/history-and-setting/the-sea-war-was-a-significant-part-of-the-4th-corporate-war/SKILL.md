# The Sea War was a significant part of the 4th Corporate War

The Sea War was a significant part of the 4th Corporate War. Both Militech and Arasaka attempted to block each other's global shipping lanes by spreading bioplagues in major seaports, and open warfare on shipping, using submersible and small warship fleets. Autonomous sea weapons caused additional destruction, leading to a global economic crisis due to the halted trans-shipping trade. One unintended result was ghost fleets left drifting, later scavenged during the Time of the Red. Attempts to establish dominance also resulted in the destruction of most OTEC and CINO assets, ironically the conflict's original cause.
