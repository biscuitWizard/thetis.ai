# The Second Corporate War (2008-2010) erupted due to failed merger talks between

The Second Corporate War (2008-2010) erupted due to failed merger talks between Petrochem and SovOil. The conflict was notable for use of military tactics by corporations, defying international governance, and left severe environmental damage, particularly polluting the southern Pacific Rim.
