# The year 2013 saw the development of the Soulkiller virus by Arasaka,

The year 2013 saw the development of the Soulkiller virus by Arasaka, capable of implanting recorded personalities into cloned bodies. It initially failed but was later weaponized. Additionally, this year marked the creation of the first true Artificial Intelligence.
