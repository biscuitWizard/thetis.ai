# Continental Brands emerged as an independent entity after severing ties with Petrochem

Continental Brands emerged as an independent entity after severing ties with Petrochem. This strategic move was led by Olivia Forsythe and Lewis McAllister, who managed to bring significant portions of Petrochem's American Agribusiness into their own operations. The shift resulted in Continental Brands becoming a major player in the food industry, liberated from Petrochem's monopolistic control.
