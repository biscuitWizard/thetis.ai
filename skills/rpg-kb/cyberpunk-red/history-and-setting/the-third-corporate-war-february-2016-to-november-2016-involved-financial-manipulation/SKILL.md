# The Third Corporate War (February 2016 to November 2016) involved financial manipulation

The Third Corporate War (February 2016 to November 2016) involved financial manipulation and net warfare between the Rothstein Fund and MA&F, eventually spilling into physical conflicts. It ended with the dissolution of the Rothstein Fund, largely conducted in the NET with significant cyber and physical battles.
