# Petrochem, headquartered in Dallas, is a major player in the global CHOOH²

Petrochem, headquartered in Dallas, is a major player in the global CHOOH² and oil markets, controlling extensive agricultural land. During the 4th Corporate War, Petrochem sold aviation fuel reserves to Militech and Arasaka, leading to increased fuel prices. Post-war, Petrochem is still a dominant force but faces challenges from companies like Continental Brands.
