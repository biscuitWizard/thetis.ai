# Central and South America experienced fragmentation during the War but are now

Central and South America experienced fragmentation during the War but are now undergoing reconstruction and alliance-building through the Organization of American States, with aid from Argentina and a newly democratic Brazil. These regions are strengthening economic ties with the Highriders and Nomads.
