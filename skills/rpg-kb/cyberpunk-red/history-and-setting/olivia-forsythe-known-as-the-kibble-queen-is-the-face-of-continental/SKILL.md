# Olivia Forsythe, known as the 'Kibble Queen', is the face of Continental

Olivia Forsythe, known as the 'Kibble Queen', is the face of Continental Brands. She represents the company as both a marketing mascot and influential executive overseeing business strategies. The Oasis stores, under Continental Brands, operate by creating community loyalty programs that reward or penalize communities based on their purchases and allegiance to the company.
