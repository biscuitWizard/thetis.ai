# Central and South America saw political realignment

Central and South America saw political realignment. Central America evolved into a unified pact of cooperative states, expelling U.S. influence aside from the Panama Canal. Conversely, South American countries like Argentina and Chile emerged as leaders of the OAS, a major world power comparable to the EU.
