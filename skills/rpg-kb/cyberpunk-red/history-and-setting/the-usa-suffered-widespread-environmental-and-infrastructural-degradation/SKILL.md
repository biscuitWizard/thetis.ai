# The USA suffered widespread environmental and infrastructural degradation

The USA suffered widespread environmental and infrastructural degradation. Sloppy pollution controls resulted in extensive acid rain and nuclear reactor leaks. Abortive orbital strikes and waste spills further damaged ecosystems, alongside the devastating Wasting Plague, carbon plague, and a significant earthquake hitting L.A. in 1999.
