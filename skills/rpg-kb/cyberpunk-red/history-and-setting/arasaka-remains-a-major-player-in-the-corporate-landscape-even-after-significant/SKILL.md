# Arasaka remains a major player in the corporate landscape even after significant

Arasaka remains a major player in the corporate landscape even after significant setbacks in the 4th Corporate War. Based in Tokyo, Japan, they have retained substantial military power and have covertly deployed security services worldwide. Within Arasaka, there are three main factions vying for control: the Kiji Faction led by Hanako Arasaka, the Taka Faction by Yorinobu Arasaka, and the Hato Faction represented by Michiko Sanderson, each with their own distinct approaches and agendas.
