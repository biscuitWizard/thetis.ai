# By 2030 to 2035, the process of resettling Night City suburbs had

By 2030 to 2035, the process of resettling Night City suburbs had begun, with many refugees occupying areas around Night City Central's radioactive ruins. Night City, once devastated, started seeing reconstruction efforts, especially in the filling of the old Bay. Nomads significantly contributed by establishing Nomad High Roads and trading routes, aiding local corporations to extend their reach regionally. Meanwhile, CitiNets, a form of isolated VPNs in reclaimed cities, began emerging under the supervision of Netwatch.
