# The harsh environmental conditions and significant ecological changes heavily impacted

The harsh environmental conditions and significant ecological changes heavily impacted the United States in the Cyberpunk universe. The melting of the polar ice caps caused a rise in sea levels, submerging parts of the East Coast and the South, turning them into swamplands. Northern areas like Alaska and Canada became field regions for bio-engineered crops after the warming temperatures, creating a new breadbasket for the economy. However, other regions such as the Great Plains and Southwest turned into deserts, with severe winds that created sandstorms, while the Pacific Northwest endured massive droughts and wildfires over two decades, resulting in dense smoke covering the West Coast.
