# Olivia Forsythe, known as the 'Kibble Queen,' heads Continental Brands

By implementing strategic loyalty programs and staving off competition, she ensures community dependence on Oasis stores through rewards and penalties based on loyalty metrics. Her rise in the corporate hierarchy is marked by ambition and tactical acumen.
