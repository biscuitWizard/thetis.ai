# Boost is another street drug, costing 50eb per dose

Boost is another street drug, costing 50eb per dose. It increases the user's INT by 2 points for 24 hours, which can exceed 8. The secondary effect, with a DV of 17, causes addiction, lowering the user's INT by 2 points when not under the influence.
