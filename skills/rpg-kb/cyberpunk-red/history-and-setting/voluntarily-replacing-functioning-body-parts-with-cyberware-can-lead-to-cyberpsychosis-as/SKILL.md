# Voluntarily replacing functioning body parts with cyberware can lead to cyberpsychosis, as

Voluntarily replacing functioning body parts with cyberware can lead to cyberpsychosis, as it involves overcoming natural resistance to self-mutilation for aesthetic or functional reasons. This does not occur with involuntary replacements, which aim to restore normalcy, such as using medical-grade cyberware for lost limbs.
