# Hanako Arasaka, the eldest daughter, leads the Kiji Faction

Hanako Arasaka, the eldest daughter, leads the Kiji Faction. She remains largely reclusive, focusing on digital projects and Netrunning. Despite her preference for these activities, she has become the figurehead for voices within the Arasaka Corporation seeking peaceful reconciliation and maintaining the corporation's traditional trajectory. Hanako also plays a key role in brokering peace within the family, including reconciling with her brother Yorinobu.
