# After the 4th Corporate War, corporate governance has changed significantly

After the 4th Corporate War, corporate governance has changed significantly. One major shift is the introduction of the 'Face' concept, where the largest stakeholder of a corporation, or 'Face,' becomes personally responsible for illegal activities the corporation commits. This rule aims to prevent the shielding of corporate leaders behind legal entities, enforcing accountability and subjecting the Face to legal penalties if malfeasance is detected.
