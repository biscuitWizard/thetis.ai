# Danger Gal, headed by Michiko Sanderson (née Arasaka), is a private investigation

Danger Gal, headed by Michiko Sanderson (née Arasaka), is a private investigation and security firm involved in high-profile cases. Though its public face is of a premium detective agency, it clandestinely works to dismantle Arasaka operations in the U.S., leveraging funds and resources provided by President Elizabeth Kress and leftover Arasaka assets.
