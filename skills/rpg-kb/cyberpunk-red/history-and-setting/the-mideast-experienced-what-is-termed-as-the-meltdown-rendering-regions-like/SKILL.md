# The Mideast experienced what is termed as the 'Meltdown,' rendering regions like

The Mideast experienced what is termed as the 'Meltdown,' rendering regions like Iran and Libya into radioactive wastelands, with only nations such as Egypt and Syria remaining intact. By the 2020s, these areas were primarily haunted by scavengers seeking valuable remnants.
