# The Transcendental Sentience AIs, feared by both Netwatch and corporations, are powerful

The Transcendental Sentience AIs, feared by both Netwatch and corporations, are powerful machine intelligences with unknown goals. However, they have a unique ability to control other digital entities within the NET. Netwatch is attempting to negotiate with these AIs, using Alt Cunningham and her digital cohort as intermediaries, to reestablish control over the Old NET in a way acceptable to both sides.
