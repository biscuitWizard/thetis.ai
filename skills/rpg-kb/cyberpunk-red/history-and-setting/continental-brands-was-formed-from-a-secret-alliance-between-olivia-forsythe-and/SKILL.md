# Continental Brands was formed from a secret alliance between Olivia Forsythe and

Continental Brands was formed from a secret alliance between Olivia Forsythe and Lewis McAllister, previously marketing directors at Continental Agricorp. They executed a strategic separation from their parent company, Petrochem, leading to the establishment of Continental Brands. This move allowed them to take control of numerous aspects of Petrochem's American agribusiness and to operate independently.
