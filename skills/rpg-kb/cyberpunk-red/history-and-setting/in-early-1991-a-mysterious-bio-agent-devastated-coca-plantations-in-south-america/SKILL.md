# In early 1991, a mysterious bio-agent devastated coca plantations in South America

In early 1991, a mysterious bio-agent devastated coca plantations in South America and poppy fields in Central Asia. This bio-agent, later revealed to be released by the Gang of Four, was part of a strategic plan to destabilize drug economies and governments in key regions, as uncovered by investigative reporter Tesla Johannesen.
