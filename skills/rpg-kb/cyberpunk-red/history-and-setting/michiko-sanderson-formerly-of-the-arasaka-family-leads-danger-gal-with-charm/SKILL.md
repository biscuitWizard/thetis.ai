Michiko Sanderson, formerly of the Arasaka family, leads Danger Gal with charm and insight. Her firm, while a legitimate investigation agency, also serves a hidden agenda to combat her family's remnants in America. Supported by her bodyguard, Kenichi Zaburo, she has maintained a complex but effective operation against Arasaka factions.
