# Cyberpsychosis is a dissociative disorder linked to excessive cyberware installations,

Cyberpsychosis is a dissociative disorder linked to excessive cyberware installations, marked by a lack of empathy and self-perception. It is induced when individuals voluntarily replace functional body parts with cybernetics, viewing themselves more as mechanical objects rather than organic beings.
