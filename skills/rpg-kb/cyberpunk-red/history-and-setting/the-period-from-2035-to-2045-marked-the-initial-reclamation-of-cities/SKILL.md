# The period from 2035 to 2045 marked the initial reclamation of cities

The period from 2035 to 2045 marked the initial reclamation of cities termed "First Wave Cities," under the aid of local governments and remaining Megacorps. Netwatch also endeavored to resuscitate the Old NET given up due to the unsuccessful battle against the R.A.B.I.D.S. Instead, the focus shifted to establishing Data Pools for open data exchange within cities. Additionally, massive structures called Mega Buildings began to rise to house the homeless.
