# Synthcoke costs 20eb per dose and has a primary effect lasting 4

Synthcoke costs 20eb per dose and has a primary effect lasting 4 hours. It increases the user's REF by 1 point, causing paranoid ideation. The secondary effect, with a DV of 15, results in addiction, which lowers REF by 2 points unless under the primary effect.
