# Futurist Africa emerges as a major space power with a significant portion

Futurist Africa emerges as a major space power with a significant portion of space construction workers hailing from African nations. The Highriders, a Pan-African group formed during the War as they declared independence, utilize their commandeered orbital weapons to maintain neutrality and sovereignty, while Africa develops advanced urban centers.
