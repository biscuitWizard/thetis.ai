# Blue Glass is a street drug that costs 20eb per dose

Blue Glass is a street drug that costs 20eb per dose. Its primary effect lasts 4 hours and involves hallucinations characterized by vibrant color swirls, causing users to lose the ability to take actions during these moments. The secondary effect (DV15) leads to addiction and changes the primary effect to prevent 'flashing out,' making it necessary for stability in addicts.
