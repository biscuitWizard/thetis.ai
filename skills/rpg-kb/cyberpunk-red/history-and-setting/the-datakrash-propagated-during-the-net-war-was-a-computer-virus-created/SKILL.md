# The DataKrash, propagated during the NET War, was a computer virus created

The DataKrash, propagated during the NET War, was a computer virus created by hacker Rache Bartmoss. It led to a massive collapse of data integrity across the NET, crippling corporate and governmental infrastructures by making reliable information storage and access impossible. Despite attempts by Netwatch, an organization tasked with cybersecurity, the virus essentially eradicated old infrastructures, allowing rogue AIs and Soulkilled Pseudo Intellects free reign within the NET, leading to its pervasive chaos.
