# Netwatch was originally established to police the NET against illegal activities but

Netwatch was originally established to police the NET against illegal activities but has been without a formal 'beat' since the War. They now have been collaborating with Transcendental AIs to secure the ruined Old NET, aiming to restore it through a project known as the Black Wall, which is highly classified and involves intense negotiation with AI entities.
