# The First Corporate War (August 2004 to February 2006) began with EBM's

The First Corporate War (August 2004 to February 2006) began with EBM's attempted buyout of Transworld Airlines, which was opposed by Orbital Air. This led to a series of attacks and shifting alliances, with acts of terrorism, piracy, and financial sabotage. The conflict was marked by two significant battles and concluded with Orbital Air capturing EBM's CEO, leading to EBM's surrender.
