# The Bodybank provides cloned limbs, reducing the demand for human cadavers

The Bodybank provides cloned limbs, reducing the demand for human cadavers. Replacement limbs are included in hospital treatments, but personal reserves of limbs can be kept for 50eb each. Cyberware from cadavers can be harvested by medtechs for resale if done properly, with installation surgeries carrying a risk of Humanity loss and varied costs based on the procedure's complexity.
