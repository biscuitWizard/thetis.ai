# Cyberpsychosis is a condition resulting from Humanity Loss, where individuals lose empathy

Cyberpsychosis is a condition resulting from Humanity Loss, where individuals lose empathy and begin to see themselves and others as mere collections of parts, leading to dissociative disorders. This mental state can develop due to excessive and voluntary body modifications with cyberware.
