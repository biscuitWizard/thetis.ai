# During the period known as "Dustbowl Hell," water scarcity became the main

During the period known as "Dustbowl Hell," water scarcity became the main challenge in western cities from Seattle to San Diego. Los Angeles suffered from poisonous fogs caused by the mixture of smog and ground fog due to lack of rain diverted by ocean currents. San Diego turned into a desert city, while Night City experienced a climatic mix of hot dry summers and acid rain. Further inland, areas like the Great Plains entirely became dustbowls, except where Petrochem maintained modified crop fields to manufacture CHOOH^2 fuel.
