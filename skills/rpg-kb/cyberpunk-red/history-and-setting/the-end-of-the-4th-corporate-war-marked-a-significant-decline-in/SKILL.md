# The end of the 4th Corporate War marked a significant decline in

The end of the 4th Corporate War marked a significant decline in the dominance of Megacorporations, but many survived by restructuring and operating on a reduced scale. While some companies dissolved or merged, localized branches of these corporations often continued to run. They adapted through strategic renaming and maintained operations wherever feasible, ensuring the continuation of some services.
