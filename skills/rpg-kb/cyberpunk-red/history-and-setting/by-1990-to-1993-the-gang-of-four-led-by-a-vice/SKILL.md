# By 1990 to 1993, the Gang of Four, led by a vice

By 1990 to 1993, the Gang of Four, led by a vice president, effectively ends federal democracy in the U.S. Many states react by declaring themselves Free States. During this period, the U.S. initiates the First Central American Conflict, driven by imperial ambitions under the guise of combating communism, terrorism, and narcotics, resulting in mass casualties and a demand for cyberware due to veterans returning home injured.
