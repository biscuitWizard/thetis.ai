# A consortium known as the Gang of Four, composed of government agencies,

A consortium known as the Gang of Four, composed of government agencies, manipulated and controlled U.S. governance and external relations. The gang comprised the Executive Branch led by the Vice President, CIA, FBI, and DEA. They engaged in war profiteering, destabilized South American governments, and released bio-agents targeting drug crops to sabotage economies and favored Megacorps interests. This cabal was exposed and dismantled following years of corruption and manipulation.
