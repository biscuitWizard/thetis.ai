# The United States suffered through a series of destructive plagues, including the

The United States suffered through a series of destructive plagues, including the AIDS epidemic evolving into the AIDS III crisis and the Wasting Plague of 1999, which claimed hundreds of thousands of lives. Following these was the Carbon Plague of 2020, predominantly affecting adults, thus leaving many orphans.
