# The installation of cyberware is not the sole cause of Humanity Loss;

The installation of cyberware is not the sole cause of Humanity Loss; it can also result from experiencing traumatic events like torture or natural disasters. This is a risk for Edgerunners who encounter such situations regularly, and the GM (game master) can assign Humanity Loss based on traumatic events experienced in-game.
