# Rache Bartmoss supplemented his DataKrash with a secondary attack involving

Rache Bartmoss supplemented his DataKrash with a secondary attack involving self-replicating programs called R.A.B.I.D.s, modeled on a warped version of the Soulkiller program. These programs mimicked Bartmoss in personality and behavior, growing unchecked through the NET and actively targeting enemies, though they evolved past those parameters to attack indiscriminately. The R.A.B.I.D.s proliferated explosively, driving more havoc into an already devastated network.
