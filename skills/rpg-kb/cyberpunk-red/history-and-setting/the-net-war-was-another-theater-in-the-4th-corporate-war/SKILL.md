# The NET War was another theater in the 4th Corporate War

The NET War was another theater in the 4th Corporate War. Militech took preventive action against Arasaka's feared Soulkiller program but faced massive disruptions from the DataKrash, a viral plague created by Rache Bartmoss that reshaped the information structure globally. Bartmoss's backdoor spread the DataKrash into every computer system connected to the NET, making data unreliable and collapsing governmental and corporate structures. The chaos was so immense that standard remedies were ineffective, sabotaging safe and organized cyber operations worldwide.
