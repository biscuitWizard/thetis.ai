# The 4th Corporate War began in 2021 when IHAG, a Megacorp specializing

The 4th Corporate War began in 2021 when IHAG, a Megacorp specializing in underwater shipping and technology, went bankrupt. This led rival ocean-based Megacorps, CINO and OTEC, to engage in a hostile takeover for IHAG's assets, initially through stock manipulation and economic warfare, but escalating into outright warfare. The war involved two major private militaries: Militech, U.S.-based, and Arasaka, Japanese-based. Their involvement escalated the conflict from corporate espionage to extreme physical altercations, overshadowing the initial IHAG asset conflict.
