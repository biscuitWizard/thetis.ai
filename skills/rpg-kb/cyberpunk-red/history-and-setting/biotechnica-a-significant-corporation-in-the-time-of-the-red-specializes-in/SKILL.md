# Biotechnica, a significant corporation in the Time of the Red, specializes in

Biotechnica, a significant corporation in the Time of the Red, specializes in genetic engineering and biochemical research. The company was pivotal during the fuel crisis with its development of CHOOH2™, a biofuel that led to worldwide changes in fuel usage. Despite their small size relative to larger megacorps, Biotechnica wields significant influence due to its innovative outputs led by Chief Officer Nicolo Logaggia.
