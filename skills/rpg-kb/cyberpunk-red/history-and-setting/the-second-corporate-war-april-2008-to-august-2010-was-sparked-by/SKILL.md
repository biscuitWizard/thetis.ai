# The Second Corporate War (April 2008 to August 2010) was sparked by

The Second Corporate War (April 2008 to August 2010) was sparked by a failed merger and an accident between Petrochem and SovOil. It escalated into a large-scale conflict, heavily impacting the Pacific Rim and resulting in significant environmental damage and disregard for national sovereignty.
