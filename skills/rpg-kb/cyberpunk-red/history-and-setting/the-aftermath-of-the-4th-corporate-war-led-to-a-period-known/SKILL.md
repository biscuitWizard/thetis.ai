# The aftermath of the 4th Corporate War led to a period known

The aftermath of the 4th Corporate War led to a period known as the Time of the Red, marked by red-tinged skies caused by atmospheric particles from nuclear blasts and other war-related debris. This period lasted for nearly two years with the skies gradually transitioning to brilliant red sunrises and sunsets over the next decade.
