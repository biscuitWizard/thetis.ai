# During the Time of the Red, Arasaka Corporation faces internal power struggles

During the Time of the Red, Arasaka Corporation faces internal power struggles among three primary factions. The Kiji Faction, led by Hanako Arasaka, attempts to maintain the corporation according to the vision of Saburo Arasaka. The Taka Faction, under Yorinobu Arasaka, Saburo's second son, seeks to overthrow the current leadership. The Hato Faction, centered on Michiko Sanderson, serves as a potential figure of legitimacy should they ascend to power. Each faction navigates the political landscape while vying for control of the corporation.
