# The 4th Corporate War significantly weakened the superpower status of the United

The 4th Corporate War significantly weakened the superpower status of the United States, a fate shared by Europe and the Far East. The remaining U.S. military forces, organized as Combined Operations Groups, often broke away to function as private armies or mercenaries post-war.
