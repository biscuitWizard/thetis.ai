# The maritime Nomads, known as 'Pirates' or 'Ship Rats,' evolved from sea

The maritime Nomads, known as 'Pirates' or 'Ship Rats,' evolved from sea pirates pre-War to protectors of cargo ships under corporate contracts. However, the chaos of the 4th Corp War forced many to become autonomous operators, eventually managing fleets like the land-based smuggling families.
