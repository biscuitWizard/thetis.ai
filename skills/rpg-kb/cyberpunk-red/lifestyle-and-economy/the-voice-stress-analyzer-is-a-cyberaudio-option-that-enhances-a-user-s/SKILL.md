# The Voice Stress Analyzer is a cyberaudio option that enhances a user's

The Voice Stress Analyzer is a cyberaudio option that enhances a user's ability to detect lies by adding +2 to their Human Perception and Interrogation skills. It requires a Cyberaudio Suite and beeps when detecting a lie during a lie-detecting function. However, it may produce false positives and negatives and additional installations do not provide extra benefits.
