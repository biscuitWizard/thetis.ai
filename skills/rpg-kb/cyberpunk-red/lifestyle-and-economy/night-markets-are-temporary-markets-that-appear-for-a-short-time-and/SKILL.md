# Night Markets are temporary markets that appear for a short time and

Night Markets are temporary markets that appear for a short time and offer various goods, which can range from food and drugs to cyberware and fashion. They do not have permanent locations, prompting buyers to rely on local Fixers for information on when and where they might appear.
