# A Linear Frame is a powered exoskeleton that increases the user's BODY

A Linear Frame is a powered exoskeleton that increases the user's BODY stat, enhancing strength without impacting HP or Death Save. The Beta variant increases BODY to 14 requiring two Interface Plugs, while the Sigma increases to 12 with one plug.
