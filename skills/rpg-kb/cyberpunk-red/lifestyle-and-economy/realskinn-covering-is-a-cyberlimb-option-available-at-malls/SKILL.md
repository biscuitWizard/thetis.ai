# Realskinn™ Covering is a Cyberlimb option available at malls

Realskinn™ Covering is a Cyberlimb option available at malls. It provides an artificial skin coating for cyberlimbs, requiring a Cyberarm or Cyberleg but not occupying an option slot. The cost is 500 eb with no humanity loss.
