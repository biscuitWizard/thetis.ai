# The Stun Baton is a premium exotic medium melee weapon costing 100eb

The Stun Baton is a premium exotic medium melee weapon costing 100eb. It cannot cause critical injuries or ablate armor. If it reduces a target to below 1 HP, the target is unconscious but stabilized at 1 HP.
