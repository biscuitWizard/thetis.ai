# Dragon is a type of Anti-Program Black ICE

Dragon is a type of Anti-Program Black ICE. It comes with a high REZ of 30 and notable stats such as PER 6, SPD 4, ATK 6, and DEF 6. It deals 6d6 damage to a Program, and if this damage is sufficient to Derezz the Program, it is instead destroyed permanently. This powerful ICE is very expensive, priced at 1,000eb. Its icon is a golden-scaled dragon robot surrounded by electrical discharges.
