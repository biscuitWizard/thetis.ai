# Smart Ammunition costs 500eb and is available for arrows, bullets, and rockets

Smart Ammunition costs 500eb and is available for arrows, bullets, and rockets. It requires targeting scope cyberware and offers a second chance to hit if a shot misses by 4 or less. This is done by matching the shot difficulty value on the range table and adding 10 to the d10 result, excluding when using LUCK. Targets can attempt to dodge this second shot normally.
