# Night Markets function as crucial hubs in the new economy, appearing spontaneously

Night Markets function as crucial hubs in the new economy, appearing spontaneously to sell recovered or transported goods by Nomads and Fixers. They embody a fluid and dynamic marketplace, with access often depending as much on one's connections and local knowledge as on actual currency.
