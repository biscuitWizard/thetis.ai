# Killer is another form of Anti-Program Black ICE, featuring stats like PER

Killer is another form of Anti-Program Black ICE, featuring stats like PER 4, SPD 8, ATK 6, and DEF 2, with a REZ of 20. It deals 4d6 damage to a Program, and similarly to the Dragon, if this damage is sufficient to Derezz the Program, the Program is destroyed. Priced at 500eb, it is expensive. The Killer is visualized as a robotic samurai with red eyes wielding a glowing katana.
