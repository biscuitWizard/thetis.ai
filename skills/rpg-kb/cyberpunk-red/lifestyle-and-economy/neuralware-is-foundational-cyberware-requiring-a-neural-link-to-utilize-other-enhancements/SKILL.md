# Neuralware is foundational cyberware requiring a Neural Link to utilize other enhancements

Neuralware is foundational cyberware requiring a Neural Link to utilize other enhancements like Braindance Recorders and Sandevistan. Installations can cost around 500eb and have variable Humanity Loss impacts, affecting player capabilities significantly.
