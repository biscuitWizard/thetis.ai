# Cyberarms are replacement limbs that come with four Option Slots for further

Cyberarms are replacement limbs that come with four Option Slots for further modifications, like Cyberarm Options or Cyberlimb Options. They include a Standard Hand, which doesn't incur Humanity Loss or take up an option slot.
