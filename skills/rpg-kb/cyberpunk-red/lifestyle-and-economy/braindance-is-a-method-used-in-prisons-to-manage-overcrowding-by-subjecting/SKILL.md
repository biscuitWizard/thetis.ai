# Braindance is a method used in prisons to manage overcrowding by subjecting

Braindance is a method used in prisons to manage overcrowding by subjecting inmates to periods of cryogenic sleep, where they experience sensory deprivation in a state akin to total isolation. This method can be more feared than execution among prisoners due to the horror of continuous inactivity and isolation, which keeps their minds active in a dark void despite being in cryogenic stasis.
