# The concept of scarcity driving the 'New Street Economy' is challenged by

The concept of scarcity driving the 'New Street Economy' is challenged by the existence of plentiful resources like water, food, and technology. However, these resources are only accessible to those who have the means and connections, such as through Fixers who facilitate their distribution.
