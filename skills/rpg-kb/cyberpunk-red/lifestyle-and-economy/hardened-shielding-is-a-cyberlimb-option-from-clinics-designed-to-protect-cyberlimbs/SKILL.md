# Hardened Shielding is a Cyberlimb option from clinics designed to protect cyberlimbs

Hardened Shielding is a Cyberlimb option from clinics designed to protect cyberlimbs and their options from being disabled by EMP effects or Non-Black ICE Program effects. It requires a Cyberarm or Cyberleg for installation, costs 1,000 eb, and causes 3 humanity loss.
