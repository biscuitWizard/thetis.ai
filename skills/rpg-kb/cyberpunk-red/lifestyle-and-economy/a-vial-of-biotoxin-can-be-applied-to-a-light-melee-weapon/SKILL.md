# A Vial of Biotoxin can be applied to a light melee weapon,

A Vial of Biotoxin can be applied to a light melee weapon, causing anyone hit within 30 minutes to attempt a DV15 Resist Torture/Drugs check to avoid 3d6 HP damage, unaffected by armor.
