# Netrunners, Fixers, and Nomads play crucial roles in the new street economy

Netrunners, Fixers, and Nomads play crucial roles in the new street economy. Netrunners locate valuable data on goods, Fixers are intermediaries who manage and sell goods, while Nomads, who have risen to power, transport and protect these goods in both aquatic and land roles.
