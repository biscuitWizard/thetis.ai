# Image Enhance is a Cybereye upgrade that grants a +2 bonus to

Image Enhance is a Cybereye upgrade that grants a +2 bonus to Perception, Lip Reading, and Conceal/Reveal Object Skills, when paired with two Cybereyes. No additional benefits occur with multiple installations.
