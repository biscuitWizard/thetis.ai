# The Master Armor List includes different types of armor with associated Damage

The Master Armor List includes different types of armor with associated Damage Stopping Power (SP), penalties, and costs. Armor such as Leathers and Kevlar® do not impose movement penalties, but heavier armor like Medium Armorjack and Flak reduce reflexes, dexterity, and movement.
