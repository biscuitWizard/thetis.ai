# The Helix is an Exotic Assault Rifle requiring the Autofire Skill, consuming

The Helix is an Exotic Assault Rifle requiring the Autofire Skill, consuming 20 bullets per attack, and cannot make Aimed or single shots. It deals damage by rolling 2d6 multiplied by the difference from the DV, up to a max of 5. Reloading takes two Actions over two turns. Required BODY for firing is 11 or higher unless mounted.
