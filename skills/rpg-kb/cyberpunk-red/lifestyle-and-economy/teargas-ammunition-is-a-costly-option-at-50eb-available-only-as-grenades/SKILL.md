# Teargas Ammunition is a costly option at 50eb, available only as grenades

Teargas Ammunition is a costly option at 50eb, available only as grenades. It deals no direct damage but forces targets with exposed eyes to make a DV13 Resist Torture/Drugs Check. Failure results in the Damaged Eye Critical Injury for 1 minute.
