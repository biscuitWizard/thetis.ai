# The Wisp is a type of Anti-Personnel Black ICE

The Wisp is a type of Anti-Personnel Black ICE. It has stats including PER 4, SPD 4, ATK 4, DEF 2, and REZ 15. Its effect is to inflict 1d6 damage directly to the enemy Netrunner's brain and to reduce the number of NET Actions the Netrunner can accomplish on their next Turn by 1, with a minimum of 2. It costs 50eb and is considered costly. It is depicted as an orb of light with a single minuscule eye crackling with energy.
