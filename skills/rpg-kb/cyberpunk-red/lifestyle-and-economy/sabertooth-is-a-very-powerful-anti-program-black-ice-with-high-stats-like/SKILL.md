# Sabertooth is a very powerful Anti-Program Black ICE with high stats like

Sabertooth is a very powerful Anti-Program Black ICE with high stats like PER 8, SPD 6, ATK 6, and DEF 2, and a REZ of 25. It inflicts 6d6 damage on a Program, and if the damage would be enough to Derezz it, the Program is destroyed instead. Sabertooth costs 1,000eb and is very expensive. Its icon is a large hulking cat with glowing white tusks.
