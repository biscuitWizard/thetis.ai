# Low Light/Infrared/UV upgrade for Cybereyes requires two eyes paired and two option

Low Light/Infrared/UV upgrade for Cybereyes requires two eyes paired and two option slots per eye. It reduces the effect of darkness and obscurants like fog, but can't see through cover.
