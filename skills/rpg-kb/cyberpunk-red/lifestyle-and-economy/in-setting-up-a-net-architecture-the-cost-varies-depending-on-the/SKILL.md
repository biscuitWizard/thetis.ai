# In setting up a NET Architecture, the cost varies depending on the

In setting up a NET Architecture, the cost varies depending on the number of floors. A setup with 3 to 6 floors allows for portable architectures and costs 1,000eb per floor. A larger, 7 to 12 floor setup is non-portable and costs 5,000eb per floor. The largest option, with 13 to 18 floors, is also non-portable and comes at a super luxury cost of 10,000eb per floor.
