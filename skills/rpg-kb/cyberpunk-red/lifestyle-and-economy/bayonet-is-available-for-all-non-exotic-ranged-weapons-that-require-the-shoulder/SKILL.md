# Bayonet is available for all non-exotic ranged weapons that require the Shoulder

Bayonet is available for all non-exotic ranged weapons that require the Shoulder Arms skill. When attached, it functions as a light melee weapon but cannot be concealed under clothing. It is priced at 100eb, categorized as premium.
