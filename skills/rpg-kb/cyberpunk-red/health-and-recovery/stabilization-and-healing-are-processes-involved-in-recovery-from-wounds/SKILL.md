# Stabilization and Healing are processes involved in recovery from wounds

Stabilization and Healing are processes involved in recovery from wounds. Stabilization must be achieved before natural healing can occur, with different Difficulty Values (DV) based on wound severity. Successful stabilization allows characters to recover hit points daily as they rest. Without stabilization, characters are at significant risk of death from their injuries.
