# Active Defenses are autonomous or remotely controlled units within a defended area

Active Defenses are autonomous or remotely controlled units within a defended area. They require a Demon controlling their Control Node unless manually controlled by a Netrunner. These defenses can be countered using Electronics/Security Tech Skills, enabling remote control within range.
