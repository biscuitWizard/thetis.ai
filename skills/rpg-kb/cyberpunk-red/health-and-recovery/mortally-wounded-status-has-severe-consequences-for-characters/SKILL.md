# Mortally Wounded status has severe consequences for characters

Mortally Wounded status has severe consequences for characters. When a character is Mortally Wounded, they suffer a Critical Injury every time they are damaged by an attack, which also increases their Death Save Penalty by 1. This condition necessitates immediate action to stabilize their condition to prevent death.
