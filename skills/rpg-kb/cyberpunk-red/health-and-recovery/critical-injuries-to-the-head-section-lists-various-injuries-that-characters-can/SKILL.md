# Critical Injuries to the Head section lists various injuries that characters can

Critical Injuries to the Head section lists various injuries that characters can sustain, each with specific effects and difficulties (DV) for Quick Fix and Treatment. These injuries include Lost Eye, Brain Injury, Damaged Eye, and more. Each injury impacts a character's actions or attributes, such as Ranged Attacks or Perception checks, and some also increase the Base Death Save Penalty. Treatment varies from requiring Surgery with different DV challenges to possible permanent fixes through Quick Fix solutions for some injuries.
