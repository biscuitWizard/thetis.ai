# Death Saves are a crucial mechanic when a character is mortally wounded

Death Saves are a crucial mechanic when a character is mortally wounded. At the start of each turn, mortally wounded characters must roll a Death Save (d10); rolling under their BODY stat means they survive and can take their turn. Rolling a 10 results in automatic failure. The Death Save Penalty increases with each roll, making survival progressively harder until stabilization.
