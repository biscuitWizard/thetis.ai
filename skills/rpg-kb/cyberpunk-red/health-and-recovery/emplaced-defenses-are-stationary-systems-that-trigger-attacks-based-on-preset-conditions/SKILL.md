# Emplaced Defenses are stationary systems that trigger attacks based on preset conditions

Emplaced Defenses are stationary systems that trigger attacks based on preset conditions. Unlike Active Defenses, they do not require a Demon for operation and use a Combat Number for attacks. They are also susceptible to countering via Electronics/Security Tech Skills.
