# Replacement Parts are available for characters who lose a body part due

Replacement Parts are available for characters who lose a body part due to Critical Injuries. Standard medical practices offer cloned tissue replacements or medical-grade cyberware without causing Humanity loss. Full cybernetic replacements require additional costs for both treatment and the cyberware itself.
