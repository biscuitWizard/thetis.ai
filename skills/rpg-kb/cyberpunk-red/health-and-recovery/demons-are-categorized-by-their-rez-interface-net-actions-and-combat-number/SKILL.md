# Demons are categorized by their REZ, Interface, NET Actions, and Combat Number

Demons are categorized by their REZ, Interface, NET Actions, and Combat Number. For example, the Imp has a REZ of 15, Interface of 3, 2 NET Actions, and a Combat Number of 14. Similarly, the Efreet and Balron demons have their own unique stats and icon descriptions.
