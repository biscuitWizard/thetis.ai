# D20 attack rolls

Choose a legal target and roll a twenty-sided die. Add the ability modifier appropriate to the attack and proficiency when the attacker is trained with the weapon or method. The attack hits if the total equals or exceeds the target's Armor Class. On a hit, roll the attack's damage; on a miss, it deals none unless a specific feature says otherwise.
