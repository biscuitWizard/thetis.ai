# D20 initiative and turns

At the start of a fight, each combatant rolls a D20 and adds the ability modifier used for alertness and speed. Act from highest total to lowest; the referee resolves ties consistently. A round ends after every participant has had a turn, then the same order repeats until the conflict ends or an effect changes it.
