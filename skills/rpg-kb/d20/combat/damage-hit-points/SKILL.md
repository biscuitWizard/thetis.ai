# D20 damage and hit points

Roll the damage specified by the successful attack, apply any resistance or vulnerability once, and subtract the result from hit points. Healing cannot raise current hit points above the maximum. At zero hit points, an ordinary foe is defeated; an important character follows the campaign's incapacitation or survival procedure. Damage below zero does not create negative hit points unless a specific rule uses them.
