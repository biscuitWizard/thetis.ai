# D20 movement and positioning

A combatant can move up to its speed during its turn and may split that movement around other actions. The referee tracks position at the precision useful to the scene. Difficult ground costs extra movement, solid barriers block travel, and occupied or dangerous areas may require a check or special permission. Cover and reach follow the established positions.
